# Wishlist and Following layout

My wishes shows family members together at the top, people before pets. Your own named-list wishes and personal wishes come first; personal wishes are grouped by list and Private/Shared audience. List administration is under Manage my lists. Family wishes follow, grouped by list with people before pets, showing a compact preview and View all. Family-space shortcuts remain available below.

The family View all dialog searches names, members, lists and stores, and filters by member, list/occasion, category, sharing and minimum/maximum price. It uses existing permission-checked list/item and personal-wish APIs. It does not grant anyone access to another adult's private personal wishes. Family-only lists are visible where existing family access permits them; followers receive only their approved shared data.

Following retains family-photo cards and member cards. Opening a family now also offers View all gifts across its approved member profiles, using the same list/member/category/budget browser. Pets sort after people. Existing family-collection cards, per-person price requests, gift saving, holds and purchase actions remain available.

Discover remains the place to search for new store products and browse retailers. No database migration or production data change is required by this layout adjustment. No changes were pushed during this task.
