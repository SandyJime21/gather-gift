# Account deletion and family succession

The red **Delete account** action is at the very bottom of Settings. It first loads a server-generated preview naming each owned family and the adult who will inherit it. It also identifies families with no successor that will be deleted. The current password and a second confirmation are required. The final **Yes** button is on the left; **No, keep my account** is on the right and receives focus.

An eligible successor must have a separate adult account, a claimed adult profile in the same family, and a family-member or administrator membership. Pending invitations, unclaimed profiles, shoppers, child accounts and support accounts cannot inherit. Priority is administrator, guardian, then oldest membership (with membership ID breaking ties).

Before confirming, an owner who does not want a transfer can cancel and remove the other adult from their family. If another eligible adult remains, they can inherit instead. If none remains, deleting the account deletes that owned family. Removing an adult from a family does not delete that adult's separate account.

Transfers and deletions run in one transaction. The successor becomes owner, gains household management and keeps existing shared profiles and wishes. Child access is reparented, previous child sessions expire, and the new owner uses their own parent PIN. Parent-created wishlist letters and family games are retained under the new owner. The departing account's claimed profiles, personal wishes, private gifting records, login and reservations are deleted. Existing families owned by other adults retain their owners.

A deletion preview expires after ten minutes and is bound to the signed-in session. Changed membership, ownership or child-management consequences require a fresh preview. Deletion requires CSRF protection, the account password, the preview token and explicit confirmation. Only the current adult family account can be deleted through these endpoints; there is no target-user argument.

Endpoints: GET /api/auth/account-deletion; DELETE /api/auth/account. No database migration is needed. No real account was deleted while implementing this feature. Automated backend tests use disposable SQLite databases with foreign-key enforcement enabled; frontend tests cover named successors, both confirmation steps, default No focus and cancellation. Production MySQL execution has not been tested with real account deletion.

When a family has no successor, its unshared children and pets, wishlists, items, occasions, wishlist letters, games, holds, saved-item references, notifications, invitations, and access grants cascade out with it. Deleting the account also removes its favorites, private gift plans, follows, budgets, support/chat records and login recovery records through their foreign keys. Unused profile, family, gift, private-gift and child theme uploads are cleaned up after commit; images still referenced by surviving records or shared artwork are preserved. Minimal audit event metadata remains with the deleted actor reference cleared.

Before deleting records, the server generates an Excel-compatible UTF-8 CSV in memory and sends it as an email attachment to the account email. It includes owned/managed wishlists (including empty list names), family/member names, gift names, descriptions, product links, prices, currencies, quantities, stores, the account?s currently accessible favorites, gifting holds and private gift plans. A different adult?s private wishes are excluded, and spreadsheet formula prefixes are escaped. No production export file is written. SMTP delivery failure or missing mail configuration stops deletion before any ownership transfer or cascade. After successful deletion, a dedicated page confirms the sent backup and distinguishes erased personal data from retained transferred-family data. Development outbox delivery is identified as a preview, never an inbox delivery.
